A little bit more about me

I'm a cultural worker, producer, writer, artist and arts administrator. I like to wax poetic about the world at large, the magic in the ordinary, and imagined worlds.

I'd like for this website to be home to archives of things I find fascinating, thoughts, sights, sounds and other things experienced through the senses or dreamed up in imagination.

I'm in an endless cycle of cleaning my room and hunting for four leaf clovers. I like to keep diaries, logs and archives, often unorganizedly and with a serving of spontaenaeity. (Did I spell that right?)
